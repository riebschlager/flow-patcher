Flow PatcherDesign system v0.1

Quiet chrome, loud signal.

A dark interface for patching sound, music and fluid visuals. Nodes and panels stay neutral graphite. Color is reserved for the six port types, so the first thing you read in any patch is what flows where.

01Color means data typeOnly ports and cables are saturated. Selection is near-white, never a hue.
02Shape backs up colorEach type has its own port shape and cable stroke. The graph reads in grayscale.
03Activity is visibleCables flash on events and glow with level. Any port opens a probe.
04Every number is a knobOne interaction model: drag, Shift for fine, double-click to reset, cable in to modulate.

Ports and cables

Cables are drawn beneath nodes. Idle cables sit at 55% opacity; activity brings them to full strength.
