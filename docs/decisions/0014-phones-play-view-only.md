# 0014 · Phones get Play view only in v1

Status: Accepted · 2026-10-21

## Decision
In v1, phones open patches in Play view only. Patch editing is for desktop and tablet.

## Consequences
- The editor doesn't need a phone layout.
- Play view, its pinned-knob strip and every canvas source must work well on phones. The Android phone is one of the reference devices.
